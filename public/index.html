How does it work?

When you use a VPN, your network traffic should travel through the encrypted VPN tunnel, including DNS queries used to translate domain names such as "google.com" into IP addresses.

If your VPN or system is configured incorrectly, DNS queries may bypass the VPN tunnel and be sent directly to your internet service provider. This can reveal information about your browsing activity even while the rest of your traffic appears protected.

This test generates unique random subdomains and records which DNS resolvers query the authoritative DNS server. If the detected DNS provider belongs to your ISP instead of your VPN or chosen DNS provider, your connection may be leaking DNS queries.
